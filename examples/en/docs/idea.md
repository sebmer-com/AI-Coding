# Idea: Request board

## In three sentences

- **User:** The service coordination team of 15 people.
- **Trigger:** An internal request arrives via chat or email.
- **Result:** The request appears with a status on a shared board and no longer gets lost.

## Today

Requests arrive through three channels (chat, email, in person). Whoever has time picks them up. Only the person who handled a request knows whether it is done. Follow-up questions cost an estimated two to three hours per week (assumption, not yet measured).

## Comparable solutions

| Solution | Link | Good pattern | Difference from my idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trello | https://trello.com | cards in columns, drag and drop status | too open, no required fields, no request template |
| GitHub Issues | https://docs.github.com/issues | title, description, status, history | built for software teams, too technical for service coordination |
| Jira Service Management | https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/service-management | request form and queue | powerful, but rollout and licence too big for 15 people |

## Glossary

- **Request:** an ask for support with a title and optional description.
- **Status:** open, in progress or done.
- **Board:** the shared list of all requests with a status filter.
