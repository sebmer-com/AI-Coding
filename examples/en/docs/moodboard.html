Method: define qualities, curate references, translate them into rules.

Three design rules

1 · Calm

Light surface, generous spacing, at most one shadow level. No animation except focus.

2 · Clear

The accent colour is reserved for the primary action of a view. Everything else is text or outline.

3 · Reliable

Status always as text plus colour, never colour alone. Empty states explain what happens next.
